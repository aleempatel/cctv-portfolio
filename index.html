How we work

A clear four-step process, so you always know what happens next and what it costs.

    I visit, check angles, lighting and cable routes, then list what you actually need.

  2. Design & quote

FAQ

Common questions

How long does an installation take?

A typical home system takes about a day. Shops and factories take longer. I confirm the exact timeline after the site survey.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. I visit, check angles and cable routes, and send a clear written quote with no obligation. The survey is free across Karachi. For very distant areas I only ask for the petrol cost of getting there, agreed with you before I set off. There is never a visit fee on top.

Can I watch my cameras on my phone?

Yes. Live view and playback on your phone are set up on every system I install.

Which camera brands do you work with?

Hikvision, Dahua, ZKTeco and Ezviz. I recommend the one that fits your budget and site.

What if a camera stops working later?

Call or message me on WhatsApp and I repair it on site. An annual maintenance plan gives you priority response.
